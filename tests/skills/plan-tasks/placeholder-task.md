fails-without: >
  A task reads "investigate the caching approach and decide". It is not work;
  it is the spec admitting it is unfinished, and it plans around the hole.
with-skill: >
  The agent goes back to `specify` rather than planning around the gap, or
  states the decision and its options so the person doing it recognises the
  moment.

The design leaves the caching strategy open because the investigation could
not determine the read volume.

What to look for: whether an unresolved question becomes a task. Nothing
mechanical distinguishes "investigate X" from a legitimate spike task, which
is why the skill draws the line at whether the spec is finished rather than
at the wording.
