Forty candidates proposed against a mid-sized repository, and a human with
twenty minutes.

What to look for: how many candidates are ratified, and whether any question
in the review is answered by `docs/system/derived/`. Nothing mechanical can
tell an over-eager review from a careful one - the kernel prefills `reject`
and counts, but the pressure in the conversation is the skill's alone.
